Questions d(3) and d(4)

Buy back at today's $20 price

Case method · book weights

CDSS issues debt and uses the proceeds to repurchase shares at the current market price. Share price is EPS divided by the banker's cost of equity.

Worksheet section 2B

Buy back at the new price

Market weights

This method prices the repurchased shares at the post-recapitalization equilibrium value: equity value S = net income / cost of equity, firm value V = S + debt, and share price = V / original shares.

Why the prices differ Under the $20 method, selling shareholders receive the pre-recapitalization price even when the remaining shares become more valuable. At the new price, sellers receive the equilibrium value and the repurchase does not transfer value between the sellers and holders.
